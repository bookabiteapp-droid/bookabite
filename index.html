Fees & VAT

Your total already includes 12% VAT on the (discounted) food subtotal. To confirm a reservation, BookaBite collects a 50% Reservation Fee online; the remaining 50% Remaining Balance is paid directly to the restaurant upon arrival.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Once a reservation has been confirmed and payment has been made, cancellation is subject to the applicable BookaBite and restaurant conditions. Customers may request rescheduling when available.

Cancellation: A 20% cancellation fee applies to the total order cost when a confirmed reservation is cancelled.

Rescheduling: A 2% rescheduling fee applies to the total order cost, subject to the restaurant checking availability of the new schedule.

No-shows: The Reservation Fee is forfeited.

Extensions: First hour extension is free. Additional extensions may be subject to a fee.

Terms & Conditions

By using BookaBite you agree that: a 50% reservation fee is required online to confirm a booking, with the remaining 50% paid at the restaurant; cancellations carry a 20% fee and rescheduling a 2% fee on the total order cost, when applicable; menus, prices and availability may change without notice; and account holders must be 18+ with a verified identity. Personal information is handled per the Data Privacy Act of 2012. This is a school project prototype; no real payments or data are processed.
